Overlays — Practical Implications by Brand

Safe Buy Overlay — Low Surprise Risk

Very safe / predictable: Bambu Lab, Prusament, Polymaker.

Generally safe: eSUN, Overture, Flashforge.

Value vs Premium Overlay

Premium: Prusament, Polymaker, Bambu Lab.

High value: eSUN, Overture, Elegoo.

Budget: SUNLU, Creality, Anycubic, Deeplee.

Material Strengths by Brand

PLA / PLA+ standouts: eSUN, Bambu Lab, Overture.

PETG standouts: Overture, Polymaker, eSUN.

Specialty / effect filaments: Amolen, Gizmo Dorks.

Common Buying Pitfalls

  • Deeplee is best treated as bulk / utility PLA rather than precision filament.
  • “Same brand” does not mean same quality across materials.
  • Budget brands benefit most from pre-drying.
  • Specialty filaments amplify brand weaknesses.
