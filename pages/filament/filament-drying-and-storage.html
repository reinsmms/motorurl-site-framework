Baseline Filament Drying Settings

This reference provides baseline filament drying settings by material family. Values are conservative defaults suitable for most consumer filament dryers. Brand-specific guidance may override these values.

Material FamilyTypical Drying TempTypical TimeNotes
PLA / PLA+40–45°C4–6 hAvoid >50°C; usually low moisture sensitivity.
PETG50–55°C4–6 hStringing is often moisture-related; dry before tuning retraction.
ABS / ASA60–65°C4–8 hHigher temps acceptable; store dry after drying.
TPU45–55°C4–8 hVery moisture sensitive; dry even if new.
Nylon / PA70–80°C8–12 hExtremely hygroscopic; print directly from dryer if possible.
PC70–80°C6–8 hNeeds aggressive drying; enclosure recommended.
PVA45–50°C4–6 hAbsorbs moisture rapidly; reseal immediately.
HIPS50–55°C4–6 hModerate sensitivity.

Over-Drying and Filament Lifespan

Over-drying is usually caused by excessive heat, not excessive time. Correct-temperature drying can be repeated many times without harm.

Best Practices

  • Obey safe maximum dryer temperatures.
  • Prefer short re-dries over repeated long cycles when exposure was brief.
  • Allow filament to cool inside the dryer when practical.
  • Remove moisture-sensitive filament from the printer after printing.
  • Seal with fresh desiccant after every session.
