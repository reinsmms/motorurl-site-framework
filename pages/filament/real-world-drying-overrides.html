Real-World Drying Overrides

Filament
Practical drying guidance based on real consumer dryer behavior

Quick Rule

Real-world drying starts with the dryer you actually own; on the Creality Dryer 2.0, that means PLA drying starts at 45°C because that is the minimum setting.

Purpose

These drying overrides complement baseline recommendations. They are based on consumer dryers and actual print outcomes rather than lab-perfect drying conditions.

Equipment Note

  • Creality Dryer 2.0 minimum temperature: 45°C.
  • This means PLA baseline drying will typically start at 45°C, not lower.
  • This slightly exceeds ultra-conservative recommendations but is safe for most modern spools.

PLA / PLA+

  • Recommended: 45–50°C for about 4 hours.
  • Preferred when printing immediately: 50°C for consistency.
  • Low moisture sensitivity does not mean zero moisture sensitivity.
  • Avoid prolonged exposure above 50°C to reduce spool deformation risk.

PETG

  • Recommended: 50–55°C for 4–6 hours.
  • Always dry before tuning retraction.
  • Moisture strongly affects stringing and surface finish.
  • Transparent PETG requires stricter drying for clarity.

TPU

  • Short cycle: 50°C for about 4 hours.
  • Full cycle: 50°C for 6–8 hours.
  • TPU is highly moisture sensitive.
  • Best results come from printing immediately after drying.
  • Consider short re-dry cycles after short exposure.

General Practical Notes

  • Printing immediately after drying gives the most consistent results.
  • Storage conditions often matter more than drying frequency.
  • Slightly higher temperatures within safe ranges can reduce guesswork.
  • Use geometry, chamfers, and tolerances to handle minor variability instead of over-tuning drying.
