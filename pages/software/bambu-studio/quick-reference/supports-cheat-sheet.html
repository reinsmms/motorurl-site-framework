Support Decision Cheat Sheet

Bambu Studio · Quick Reference
Fast support/no-support decision aid

Quick Rule

Ask whether the layer is printing onto something or into air. Onto something usually means no supports; into air needs evaluation.

Overhang Angle

  • 0–45°: usually safe.
  • 45–60°: borderline; depends on material, cooling, speed, and surface expectations.
  • 60°+: usually needs support.

Overhang Distance

  • Less than 2 mm: usually safe.
  • 2–5 mm: usually fine.
  • 5–10 mm: risky.
  • More than 10 mm: usually needs support.

Shape Rule

  • Gradual slopes and curves are usually safe.
  • Sudden ledges and flat ceilings usually need support.

Bridge vs Overhang

  • Overhang: grows outward from a previous layer.
  • Bridge: spans a gap between two supported areas.
  • Bridges are riskier because the filament is crossing open space.

Material

  • PLA: generally best for overhangs.
  • PETG: slightly worse than PLA and more prone to stringing or sagging.
  • ABS: generally more difficult for clean unsupported overhangs.

Red Flags

  • Flat undersides.
  • Large bridges.
  • Steep angles.
  • Any first layer that would print into air.

Green Flags

  • Threads.
  • Cones.
  • Curved surfaces.
  • Organic shapes.

Decision Flow

  • Flat underside? Add support.
  • Gradual slope? Usually no support.
  • Bridge? Evaluate span length and material.
  • Under 45°? Usually no support.
  • Very small unsupported distance? Usually no support.
