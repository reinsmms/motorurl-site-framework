Bambu A1 Filament Runout Guide

Bambu Studio · Quick Reference
What to do when filament runs out or jams at the spool end

Quick Rule

When the A1 pauses for filament runout, load the new spool, purge cleanly, wipe any nozzle blob, and resume.

Normal Scenario

The printer detects runout, pauses automatically, and parks the toolhead safely.

  1. Remove the empty spool.
  2. Mount the new spool.
  3. On the printer screen, choose Load Filament.
  4. Insert the filament into the inlet/PTFE tube.
  5. Let the printer grab and purge the filament.
  6. Wipe the nozzle tip if a blob formed during the pause.
  7. Tap Resume Print.

Spool-Hole Scenario

Some spools store the filament end in a hub hole. If the end stays trapped, the last loops may not unwind.

  1. Remove the spool.
  2. Pull the remaining filament out of the tube or extruder.
  3. Install the new spool.
  4. Choose Load Filament.
  5. Feed filament until the extruder grabs it.
  6. Allow the printer to purge.
  7. Resume the print.

Printer Won’t Grab the New Filament

This usually means a small stub of filament is still inside the extruder.

  1. Select Unload Filament.
  2. Pull out the remaining stub.
  3. Select Load Filament.
  4. Insert the new filament until the extruder grabs it.
  5. Let the printer purge.
  6. Resume the print.

Prevent Spool-End Jams

  1. Remove the filament end from the hub storage hole before installing the spool.
  2. Pull off about 30–50 cm of filament and rewind it loosely.
  3. Make sure the filament is free and not locked into any hole.
  4. Cut the filament tip at a 45° angle.
  5. Load normally.

Good Habits

  • Always check the filament end before installing a spool.
  • Clip the filament tip at a 45° angle.
  • Watch the first few seconds after resuming.
  • Remove nozzle blobs with tweezers before the print continues.
