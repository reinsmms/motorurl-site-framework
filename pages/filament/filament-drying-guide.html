Drying reference settings and notes by material family

Drying reference settings and notes by material family. Use manufacturer guidance when it conflicts with these defaults.

Baseline Filament Drying Settings

Material FamilyTypical Drying TempTypical TimeNotes / Exceptions
PLA / PLA+40–45°C4–6 hAvoid >50°C; usually low moisture sensitivity.
PETG50–55°C4–6 hDry before tuning retraction; stringing is often moisture-related.
ABS / ASA60–65°C4–8 hHigher temps acceptable; store dry after drying.
TPU45–55°C4–8 hVery moisture sensitive; dry even if new.
Nylon / PA70–80°C8–12 hExtremely hygroscopic; print directly from dryer if possible.
Polycarbonate / PC70–80°C6–8 hNeeds aggressive drying; enclosure recommended.
PVA45–50°C4–6 hAbsorbs moisture rapidly; reseal immediately.
HIPS50–55°C4–6 hLower moisture sensitivity than nylon.

PETG TypeDrying SensitivityVisual SensitivityNotes
Opaque PETGModerateLowMoisture mainly affects stringing and surface quality.

Visual Symptoms and Corrective Action

SymptomLikely CauseAction
Fine bubbles in extrusionMoistureDry 55°C +2–4 h.
Cloudy / milky clear partsMoisture or cooling too fastDry fully; reduce fan if appropriate.
Excessive stringingMoistureDry before tuning retraction.
Popping / hissing soundsSevere moistureFull drying cycle required.
Rough or pitted surfaceMoistureIncrease drying time.
Loss of glossMoistureDry and store sealed immediately.
