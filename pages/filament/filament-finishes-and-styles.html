Filament Finishes and Styles

Filament
Definitions for visual and functional filament styles

Purpose

Define filament visual and functional styles, sometimes called effects or finishes. This is independent of base material such as PLA, PETG, or TPU.

Standard Finishes

  • Matte
  • Gloss

Silk & Reflective

  • Silk
  • Dual-color / two-tone
  • Tri-color / multi-color

Gradient & Color-Shift

  • Rainbow / gradient
  • UV color-change
  • Temperature color-change

Filled / Composite Styles

  • Carbon fiber (CF)
  • Glass fiber (GF)
  • Wood fill
  • Metal fill: bronze, copper, steel
  • Marble / stone

Special Effect Styles

  • Glitter / sparkle
  • Metallic pigment
  • Glow-in-the-dark

Transparency

  • Transparent / clear
  • Translucent / frosted

Flexible Styles

  • TPU standard
  • TPU soft / high-flow variants

Notes

  • Styles affect strength, layer adhesion, abrasion, and nozzle choice.
  • Many specialty styles are aesthetic-first.
