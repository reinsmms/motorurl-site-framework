Cold Pull Procedure

Bambu Studio · Quick Reference
Quick nozzle-cleaning workflow

Quick Rule

Use PLA for the pull, heat to printing temperature, cool until the filament grips debris, then pull straight out firmly.

Quick Checklist

  1. Turn on Maintenance Mode.
  2. Load PLA filament; use a light color if possible.
  3. Heat nozzle to about 200–220°C.
  4. Purge a small amount of filament.
  5. Cool nozzle to about 125°C, or lower if your setup allows.
  6. Pull filament straight out firmly.
  7. Do not jerk sideways.
  8. Use Extrude/Retract controls or manual pull, but do not fight both aggressively at the same time.
  9. Inspect the tip for debris, dark specks, or burnt material.
  10. Repeat one or two times if needed.
  11. Turn off Maintenance Mode.

After TPU Prints

After printing TPU, repeat the cold pull process using PLA. PLA firms up better and can pull residue from the nozzle more effectively.

Why PLA Works Well

PLA becomes firm at lower temperatures and grips debris better. PETG can be used if necessary, but it remains rubbery longer and is usually less effective.

What a Cold Pull Removes

  • burnt filament
  • heat-degraded plastic
  • leftover material from previous prints
  • TPU residue

Maintenance Mode

Maintenance Mode gives manual control of temperature and extrusion and helps prevent automatic routines from interfering. It is optional, but it makes the process easier.

Pro Tip

For better feel and control, remove the PTFE tube and feed filament directly. This improves your ability to feel resistance during the pull.
